# NVIDIA (NVDA)

Semiconductor design company, headquartered in Santa Clara. Started in consumer GPUs; core business is now data-center AI accelerators (the Hopper/Blackwell series) and the CUDA software ecosystem around them, making it one of the biggest direct beneficiaries of the 2026 AI-infrastructure investment cycle. Long-term #1 or #2 by global market cap (the exact rank fluctuates with share price). CEO: Jensen Huang.
