# Apple (AAPL)

Consumer electronics and services company, headquartered in Cupertino. Core product lines are iPhone/Mac/iPad/Watch; the services business (App Store, subscriptions) has taken up a growing share of revenue in recent years. Long-term top-3 by global market cap, trading the #1 spot back and forth with NVIDIA.
