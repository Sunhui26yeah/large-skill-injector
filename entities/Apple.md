# Apple (AAPL)

Consumer electronics and services company, headquartered in Cupertino. Core product lines are iPhone/Mac/iPad/Watch; the services business (App Store, subscriptions) has taken up a growing share of revenue in recent years. Long-term top-3 by global market cap, trading the #1 spot back and forth with NVIDIA.

## Leadership

- **John Ternus became CEO on 2026-09-01**, taking over from Tim Cook after Cook's ~15-year tenure. Cook did not leave the company — he moved to **Executive Chairman** and continues to handle some policymaker engagement. Ternus came up through hardware engineering and is described internally as more of a product person with a more decisive style than Cook's operations/consensus approach; his stated early priority is AI.
